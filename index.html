Projects

BioSketch: LLM-assisted design exploration with biodata

Human–AI co-creation · LLM-based prototyping · 2026

An LLM-programming tool for non-linear, slow ideation with biodata as a design material. Used in human–AI co-creation sessions with experts and non-experts, analysed through think-aloud protocols and artifact analysis. The analysis shows how participants and LLMs make sense of biodata through metaphor and how frictions between those metaphors expose biases.

AI-enhanced interactive storytelling for children

Participatory design · High-fidelity prototyping · 2025

A co-designed interactive digital narrative prototype that supports children's executive functions and cultural learning through parent–child interaction. Evaluated with semi-structured interviews, reflexive thematic analysis and usability testing. Published as a peer-reviewed poster at HAI ’25.
